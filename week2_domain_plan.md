**Project title:**Tourism Guide Assistant (RAG)**Target user:**Travelers, tourists, and visitors (solo or families) who want quick, accurate answers about a destination and its travel planning.**Problem statement:**Users often have questions about tourist attractions, opening hours, ticket prices, best times to visit, local transport, and rules for visiting. The chatbot should answer these questions using the provided destination/travel documents so that answers are consistent, sourced, and tailored to the destination.**Documents to be used:*** Official Visitor Guide (PDF): Covers main attractions, historical sites, and seasonal events.* Transit \& Accessibility Guide (PDF/Text): Details public transport routes, airport transfers, and ticket prices.* Official Tourism FAQ (Text/MD): Answers common tourist queries copied from the official board website.* Cultural \& Safety Guide (PDF/Text): Outlines local laws, emergency numbers, dress codes, and etiquette.* Ticketing \& Timings Brochure (PDF): Lists current opening hours and entry fees for major monuments and museums.**Five expected questions:**1. What are the top attractions in \[Destination] and how much time should I allocate for each?2. What are the opening hours of \[Specific Attraction] on weekdays and weekends?3. What is the ticket price / entry fee for \[Attraction], and are there any discounts?4. When is the best time to visit \[Destination], and what weather should I expect?5. How do I get from the airport to the city, and what public transport options are available?**Questions the system should not answer:*** Questions unrelated to the chosen destination (e.g., visas for other countries, unrelated cities).* Personal advice that is not supported by documents (e.g., “Should I do this exact medical/financial action?”).* Anything that would require real-time updates not present in documents (e.g., “Did opening hours change yesterday?”).* Questions about private/sensitive information (personal data of individuals, internal documents).* Legal/medical decisions beyond general public guidance found in documents.**Success criteria:*** The chatbot answers the 5 expected questions using only the provided documents.* If an answer is not found in documents, the chatbot should say it can’t find it in the knowledge sources.* The knowledge sources are organized and are used consistently in the RAG pipeline.* The chatbot successfully retrieves accurate information from the provided tourism documents for at least 4 out of the 5 expected questions.* The chatbot declines to answer out-of-scope questions instead of hallucinating or making up information.* Responses include clear, factual information (no hallucinated details like prices/hours).
